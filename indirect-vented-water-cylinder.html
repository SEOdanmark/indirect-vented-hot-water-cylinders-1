Best indirect vented hot water cylinders for UK homes

The vertical distance between the cold water storage cistern and the indirect vented hot water cylinder creates what is known as the static head. The greater the available static head, the greater the potential gravity-fed pressure at the hot water outlets. This is one of the main characteristics separating an indirect vented water cylinder from an unvented hot water cylinder, which normally uses incoming mains water pressure.

A common requirement for an indirect vented cylinder is the inclusion of an immersion heater. An indirect vented hot water cylinder with immersion heater capability provides another method of heating the stored water. While the indirect heat exchanger coil normally receives heat from the primary external heat source, an immersion heater can provide backup or top-up heating when required.

For customers looking for an indirect vented hot water cylinder with immersion heater, a stainless steel vented indirect cylinder, an open vented indirect cylinder or a high-capacity indirect vented water tank, Flexiheat UK can provide cylinder options across a wide range of capacities and heat exchanger specifications.

UK building regulations also need to be considered. Part G requires vented hot water cylinders to have a thermostat controlling the heat source and preventing the stored water temperature from exceeding 100°C. The purpose of the indirect coil is to transfer heat efficiently into the stored domestic hot water. Heating water from the boiler or other external heat source circulates through the internal heat exchanger without mixing with the potable water stored inside the indirect vented hot water cylinder. Energy passes through the stainless steel coil and raises the temperature of the stored water.

Correct sizing is an important consideration when selecting an indirect vented hot water cylinder. The required capacity depends on factors including property size, number of occupants, bathrooms, showers, baths and the expected pattern of hot water consumption. A domestic property with moderate hot water requirements will generally require a different indirect vented water cylinder from a large house, manor house, commercial property or industrial application with substantial simultaneous hot water demand.

Insulation performance is another factor to consider when comparing indirect vented hot water cylinders. After energy has been transferred into the stored water, effective insulation helps reduce heat loss from the cylinder. Lower standing heat loss means less energy is required to maintain or restore the required water temperature.

The double extra-large coil range can also be suited to heat pump installations. Heat pumps generally work with lower heating water temperatures than conventional boilers. As a result, a larger heat exchanger surface area can be beneficial when transferring energy into the stored domestic hot water. The larger coils also provide lower pressure drops and can help extract available heat from the lower flow temperatures associated with heat pump systems.
